title: Breach Response Runbook (Synthetic)
classification: restricted
roles: ciso
---
A confirmed data breach must be escalated to the CISO within 2 hours.
The CISO decides whether regulators and affected customers are notified.
The breach bridge call opens within 45 minutes of confirmation.
Legal holds are placed on all related logs for 400 days.
