title: Incident Reporting Guide (Synthetic)
classification: internal
roles: manager, ciso
---
Any suspected security incident must be reported to the security desk within 4 hours.
Managers must open an incident ticket and record the time of discovery.
The security desk assigns a severity rating after triage.
Affected systems must not be powered off before the security desk gives approval.
A post-incident review is held within 10 business days.
